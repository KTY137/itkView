# itkView v0.2.0

This release makes a module's pictures and evidence reachable on stitched
R3–R5 modules, stops a stale mirrored verdict from holding a stage gate shut,
and adds a browser-local gate-figures-only reading of a component page.

It supersedes the prepared but never tagged `v0.1.1`, whose changes are
included here.

## Images and evidence on stitched modules

- A full R3–R5 module's direct child is a half module, and the sensors,
  powerboard and hybrid assemblies carrying the photographs and measurements
  hang off that half module. The gallery and the worksheet stopped at one hop
  and therefore showed a stitched module's half modules and nothing they are
  made of. Both now take one further hop, **only through a child that is
  itself a module**, never through a sensor's or powerboard's own children.
  On the reference mirror this raises module pages showing at least one image
  from 176 to 198 of 265, and brings 114 previously unreachable
  evidence-bearing parts onto the worksheet.
- The component list column no longer stays blank on a module row that has no
  photograph of its own. It borrows one from an assembled part and **marks it**
  — dashed edge, corner glyph, and the part named in the tile's title and alt
  text. A sensor's photograph is not a picture of the module, and an unmarked
  tile would claim exactly that. The row's own picture always wins.

## Stage gates

- A worker-confirmed upload written in the same clock tick as the mirrored row
  it supersedes could lose to that row. The tie was decided by comparing a
  `TestRunEvidence` id against an `OutboxAction` id — two unrelated sequences,
  and the evidence table is thousands of runs deep while local actions start at
  one, so the stale mirrored verdict won essentially every tie and kept the
  gate shut until the next sync. An exact tie now belongs to the confirmation,
  which is written after the row it replaces.

## Data detail

- **Account → Data detail** offers a browser-local *Gate figures only* reading
  of a component page: per stage the required test, whether it passed, failed
  or is missing, and its date. Measured values, run lists, plots, non-required
  tests and child-component evidence are left out. Nothing is deleted or
  hidden from the mirror and the full view is one click away; the choice stays
  in this browser and reaches no server.
- Mirrored images are unaffected — the reduction is about measurement data, not
  pictures.

## Read-only boundary

Unchanged: authoring, upload, staging, assembly, stage moves and PDB writes
remain unavailable in both the UI and server policy. The gate-figures reading
is additionally tied to a read-only worksheet, so it can never hide values from
someone editing them.

## Known limitations

- The Windows installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn.
- A new installation still needs a mirror sync after personal PDB credentials
  are connected under **Account**.
- The list column borrows a picture from the parts one assembly hop away only;
  a component whose pictures sit deeper still shows no tile.

## Installer integrity

The exact installer size and SHA-256 are recorded after the release build.
